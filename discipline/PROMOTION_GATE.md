# Promotion gate

The default FTS search is the baseline. The router is experimental. A candidate may be promoted only after a frozen independent evaluation, exact evidence-delivery checks, separately reviewed semantic acceptance, and a cost measurement all meet predeclared thresholds. The agent or candidate cannot score itself.

The current 24-question synthetic comparison is orientation evidence only: 20 answerable, 4 unanswerable, 25 required groups, 3-document/6,000-character/2,000-per-source bounds. Its reported method counts are not semantic acceptance, a real-vault benchmark, or a universal comparison.

Filename hits are diagnostic. `--evidence-contract` is required for exact source delivery. `evaluate.py --gate` always fails its unmeasured semantic condition. Independent acceptance must be established outside this runner; it has no acceptance-import override. Unmeasured token, latency, or end-to-end speed gains are not claims. A cheaper packet with lost evidence is a regression.
