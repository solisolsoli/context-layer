# Companion

The agent's working memory about you and your ongoing threads, written by the
agent and checked by you:

- [[Core]]: who you are and how you like to work, as you stated it.
- [[Rules]]: your corrections and preferences, dated.
- [[Last-Session]]: where the last session stopped.
- [[Threads]]: open and closed lines of work.
- [[Journal]]: observations and hypotheses, clearly labelled as such.

The binding rules live in `CLAUDE.md`; the dated record of work lives in [[LOG]].
If anything here contradicts LOG, LOG wins.
