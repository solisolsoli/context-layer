# Example - Drip Irrigation Basics

> [!note] Example note
> Fictional content that shows linking and supersession. Safe to delete.

Drip irrigation delivers water slowly at the root through a line with small
emitters. For raised beds it wastes less water than a sprinkler and keeps the
leaves dry.

- One emitter per plant, or a line with emitters every 30 cm.
- A pressure reducer and a filter go between the tap and the line.
- How often to water is a separate question: see
  [[Example - Watering Schedule v2]].
