# Projects

Work with a goal and an end. One note per project: goal, status, next step, and
links to its decisions, meetings and sources. Decision and meeting notes can live
here beside the project they belong to.

Finished or abandoned projects move to the archive (`{{folder:archive}}/`, outside
search) with a closing line that says why.
