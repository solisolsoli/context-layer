# Example - Decision - Timer Controller

> [!note] Example note
> Fictional content that shows linking and supersession. Safe to delete.

Date: 2026-01-15

## Question

Which timer should control the drip line?

## Options

1. A mechanical dial timer.
2. A battery timer with two programs.

## Decision and why

Option 2, because the schedule in [[Example - Watering Schedule v2]] needs a
second watering time on hot days.

## Evidence

- PARTIAL: the schedule note asks for morning watering every day; evening
  watering only on days above 28 C, so two programs are needed on hot days
  only.
- USER_STATED: a battery is acceptable; there is no power outlet near the beds.
- NOT_FOUND: no note in this vault gives the battery life. This search found
  nothing; it does not mean the battery life is short.
