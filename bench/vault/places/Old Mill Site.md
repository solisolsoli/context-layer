# Old Mill Site

Former wool mill on the south bank of the [[Kell River]]; closed in 1994.

- Area: 6.8 hectares.
- Arsenic hotspots were found in the made ground at the northeast corner, left by a dye house.
- The brick chimney is on the local heritage list.
