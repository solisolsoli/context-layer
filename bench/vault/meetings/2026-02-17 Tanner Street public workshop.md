# Tanner Street public workshop, 17 February 2026

About 60 residents and business owners attended at the community hall.

## What we heard
- Parents want separation from traffic near the schools.
- Residents asked for lanes of at least 2.0 m so cargo bikes can pass.
- Shop owners worried about losing loading bays.
