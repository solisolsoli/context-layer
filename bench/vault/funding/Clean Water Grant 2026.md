# Clean Water Grant 2026

Capital grant administered by the [[County Water Board]].

- Award: €2,500,000 for 2026 to 2028
- Conditions: a 25% local match, paid from the capital plan
- Progress reports are due quarterly, on the 15th of the month after each quarter ends.
- Eligible uses: works that reduce polluted runoff or flood risk.
