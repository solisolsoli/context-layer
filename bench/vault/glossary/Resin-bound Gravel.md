# Resin-bound Gravel

A surface of natural aggregate mixed with a clear, UV-stable polyurethane resin and trowelled flat. It lets rainwater drain through the voids and is smooth enough for wheelchairs and road bikes. Not to be confused with resin-bonded gravel, where loose stones are scattered onto a resin coat.
