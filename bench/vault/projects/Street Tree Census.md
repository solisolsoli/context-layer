# Street Tree Census

Count of every street and park-edge tree in the city, completed in spring 2026.

- Lead: [[Arlo Penhaligon]]
- Team: [[GIS Unit]]
- Result: 11,860 trees recorded, 212 flagged for inspection
- Current canopy cover: 21% of the city area
- Target: see [[DEC-005 Tree canopy target]]

Part of [[Projects MOC]].
