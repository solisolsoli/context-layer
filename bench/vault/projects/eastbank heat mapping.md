Street-level temperature survey of [[Eastbank]] to target shade planting. See [[Urban Heat Island]].

- Lead: [[Kasimir Lent]]
- Community partner: [[Eastbank Residents Association]]
- Volunteers carry sensors on three hot afternoons in July.
