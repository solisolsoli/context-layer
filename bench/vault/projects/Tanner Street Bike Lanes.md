# Tanner Street Bike Lanes

Cycle lanes in both directions along [[Tanner Street]], with bus stop bypasses.

## Summary
- Lead: [[Selma Rudd]]
- Team: [[Mobility Team]]
- Budget: €780,000
- Funding: [[Active Travel Fund]]
- Speed limit after the works: 30 km/h
- Lane width and separation: see [[DEC-009 Tanner Street protected lanes 2.2 m]]
