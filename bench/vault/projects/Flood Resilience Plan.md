# Flood Resilience Plan

City-wide plan for river and surface-water flooding, due to the council in 2027.

- Lead: [[Ines Vardy]]
- Consultant: [[Brightwater Hydrology]]
- Budget: €900,000 from [[Clean Water Grant 2026]]
- Measures include raised defences at [[Weir Lane]] and a further 12 bioswales on the [[Eastbank]] floodplain.

Part of [[Projects MOC]].
