# Stormwater Retrofit Program

Retrofitting streets in the old town to hold and clean rainwater before it reaches the [[Kell River]].

- Lead: [[Ines Vardy]]
- Consultant: [[Brightwater Hydrology]]
- Budget: €1,600,000 from [[Clean Water Grant 2026]]
- Scope: 38 bioswales planned, plus kerb inlets on six streets. See [[Bioswale]].
