# Tanner Road Cycle Track Concept

A 2023 concept for a two-way cycle track on Tanner Road, the ring road south of the old town. Abandoned when the ring road was resurfaced.

- Proposed width: 2.0 m two-way track
- Estimated budget: €1,100,000
- Speed limit assumed: 50 km/h

Part of [[Projects MOC]].
