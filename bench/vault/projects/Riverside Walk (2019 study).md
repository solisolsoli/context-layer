# Riverside Walk (2019 study)

Feasibility study for a riverside path, commissioned after the 2019 flood and never built.

- Proposed length: 3.6 km along the south bank
- Proposed surface: asphalt with a concrete edge strip
- Estimated cost: €1,900,000, with a contingency of 20%
- Outcome: shelved in 2020 for lack of funding.

Part of [[Projects MOC]].
