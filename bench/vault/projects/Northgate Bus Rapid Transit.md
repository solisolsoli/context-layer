# Northgate Bus Rapid Transit

A rapid bus line from [[Northgate Station]] to the harbour, delivered with the [[Kellbrook Transit Authority]].

## Summary
- Project lead: [[Tobias Quell]]
- Signal engineering: [[Rafe Duncombe]]
- Budget: €12,400,000, funded by the transit authority
- [[Transit Signal Priority]] at 11 intersections
- Lane layout: [[DEC-010 Northgate BRT centre-running lanes]]
