# Riverside Greenway

Walking and cycling route along the north bank of the [[Kell River]].

## Summary
- Status: under construction since January 2026
- Lead: [[Maren Holloway]]
- Drainage advice: [[Ines Vardy]]
- Team: [[Green Infrastructure Team]]
- Length: 4.2 km, from [[Weir Lane]] to the harbour
- Funding: [[Active Travel Fund]]
- Construction contract: €2,600,000 with [[Fennick and Rowe Landscape]]

## Budget
- Total budget: €3,100,000
- Contingency: 12%
