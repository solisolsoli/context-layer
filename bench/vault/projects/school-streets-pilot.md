# School Streets pilot

Timed closures to motor traffic outside two primary schools near [[Tanner Street]].

- Lead: [[Selma Rudd]]
- Engagement: [[Juno Achterberg]]
- Budget: €145,000
- Funding: [[Active Travel Fund]]
- Closure times: 8:15 to 9:00 and 14:45 to 15:30 on school days.

Part of [[Projects MOC]].
