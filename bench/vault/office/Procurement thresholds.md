# Procurement thresholds

Rules for buying works, goods and services.

| Value | Route |
| --- | --- |
| up to €25,000 | one written quote |
| €25,000 to €140,000 | three written quotes |
| above €140,000 | open tender |

- An open tender is required above €140,000.
- Contracts above €250,000 must be signed by the [[Wendell Ferro|Planning Director]].
- Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is not allowed.
