# DEC-011 Greenway surface resin-bound gravel

## Decision
The greenway surface will be [[Resin-bound Gravel|resin-bound gravel]], 3.5 m wide, to cut runoff into the river.

## Context
Proposed at the [[2026-03-10 Greenway steering group]]. This replaces [[DEC-004 Greenway surface asphalt]]. Cost increase of €140,000 covered by the contingency.

Listed in [[Decision Log]].
