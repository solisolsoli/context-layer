# DEC-008 Old Mill affordable share 30 percent

## Decision
At least 30% of the homes on the [[Old Mill Site]] must be affordable, half of them for social rent.

## Context
Decided on 2026-04-09 at the [[2026-04-09 Old Mill steering group]], after the housing grant was confirmed. This replaces [[DEC-002 Old Mill affordable share 20 percent]].

Listed in [[Decision Log]].
