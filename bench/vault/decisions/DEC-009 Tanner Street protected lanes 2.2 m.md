# DEC-009 Tanner Street protected lanes 2.2 m

## Decision
Cycle lanes on [[Tanner Street]] will be 2.2 m wide and kerb-protected, with a 0.5 m buffer. Forty parking bays are removed.

## Context
Agreed at the [[2026-03-03 Tanner Street design check-in]] after the public workshop. This replaces [[DEC-003 Tanner Street lane width 1.8 m]].
