# DEC-012 Parking meter hours 9 to 20

## Decision
Pilot meters charge from 9:00 until 20:00, Monday to Saturday. Sundays are free.

## Context
Follows the [[2026-05-06 Parking pilot retrospective]]; evening demand was higher than expected. This replaces [[DEC-006 Parking meter hours 8 to 18]].
